Three graphic styles from the mood board, drawn by code and placed in the five sections that need artwork. Shuffle grows a new variation of every graphic. Colour buttons are a quick preview only; colours are the next step.

A · Flow linesLines flow from many sources into one node, our validator. Nature and network at once. Ref: the blue field-line drawing.
B · StipplePolyps and fronds drawn in dots, like a halftone print. Each dot could stand for corals planted. Ref: the dotted flowers, and PR #15.
C · BranchingCoral grown by a branching rule, over a flat disc. The most literal coral, and the calmest. Ref: the branching trees on beige.
