FAQ

Straight answers.

How do I get my SOL back?

Swap refiSOL back to SOL through the widget, any time. No waiting period. The rate depends on market liquidity at that moment.

I already stake somewhere else. How do I switch?

If you hold a liquid staking token (jitoSOL, mSOL, bSOL…), swap it straight to refiSOL — no unstaking wait. If you stake natively, deactivate your stake (it unlocks at the end of the epoch, up to ~2 days), then swap the SOL to refiSOL here. Rewards continue from the moment you hold refiSOL.

What are the risks?

Smart-contract risk on the stake pool and the swap route. Validator downtime would lower yield but never touches your principal. refiSOL can trade slightly above or below its underlying value. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of return.

What are the fees?

5% of staking rewards. That's the validator fee. There's no deposit fee. Normal swap fees apply when entering or exiting.

Where does the yield come from?

From Solana itself: network staking rewards plus transaction tips (we run the Jito client, which adds MEV tips on top of base rewards). The impact projects are funded by our fee. They are not a source of yield, and not a risk to it.

Does all staking with ReFi Hub fund impact?

No. Only refiSOL routes fees to impact projects. If you delegate to our validator another way, we earn a fee but it doesn't fund restoration. refiSOL is the only impact route.

How do I verify the impact?

Donations move on-chain. The transfer history is linked in the Proof section. SeaTrees publishes its own planting and project records at seatrees.org.
